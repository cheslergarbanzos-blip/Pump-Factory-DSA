OUR COMMUNITY

More than a climbing gym.

We are a welcoming space for movement, connection, and adventure. Pump Factory gives climbers of every level room to challenge themselves, learn new skills, and enjoy the climb together.

Built for every climber

Pump Factory started with a simple idea: climbing should feel like home. What began as a small bouldering space in Molo, Iloilo City has grown into a full climbing and fitness community, built by people who fell in love with the wall and wanted to share that feeling with others.

Our routes are designed to make your first climb approachable and your next project exciting. Whether you come in to train, relax, or meet new people, our team is here to help you feel at home on the wall.

Over the years, we've hosted competitions, community nights, and certificate courses that brought climbers of all backgrounds together under one roof. Every event, route reset, and renovation has been guided by feedback from the people who climb with us every day.

We believe climbing should be active, social, and accessible. That is why we pair thoughtful routes with a supportive community and programs that help you keep growing, no matter where you are in your climbing journey.

Our Mission, Vision & Values

Mission

To make climbing approachable for everyone by providing safe, well-maintained routes, knowledgeable coaching, and a community that welcomes climbers of every level.

Vision

To be Iloilo's home for climbing culture, where people come not just to train, but to belong, grow, and push their limits together.

Values

Safety, inclusivity, and community drive everything we do, from how we set our routes to how we welcome first-time climbers through our doors.
