FAQ

What is bouldering?

Bouldering is climbing on shorter walls with padded floors, so there are no ropes or harnesses. It is a fun, social workout for all experience levels.

How do I get started with bouldering?

No experience is needed. Start with an easy route, warm up at your own pace, and ask our team for tips whenever you need them.

What should I wear or bring?

Wear comfortable activewear that lets you move freely. Bring water and socks for rental shoes; everything else you need to begin is available at Pump Factory.
