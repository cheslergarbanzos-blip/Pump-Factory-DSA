FAQ

What should I wear for my first time?

Wear stretchy, comfortable athletic clothes like leggings, shorts, or a t-shirt that allow a full range of motion.

Do I need a lot of upper body strength?

Good technique, core stability, and footwork matter much more than being able to do endless pull-ups.

What is the difference between bouldering and top-roping?

Bouldering takes place on shorter walls over thick padded floors without ropes, requiring only shoes and chalk. Top-roping involves climbing taller walls with a harness and a rope secured by a safety partner or an auto-belay system.

How tight should climbing shoes actually be?

Youer shoes should be snug with your toes slightly curved, leaving no dead space or air pockets around the heel or arch.

What are climbing grades and how do the work?

Bouldering uses the V-scale (starting at V0 for beginners and going up to V17 for professionals).

What is the most important technique for a beginner?

Keep your armss straight as much as possible to hang your weight on your skeletal structure rather than exhausting your muscles.

How often should a beginner train?

Start by climbing 2 to 3 times per week with at least one full day of rest between sessions.

What basic etiquette sould I follow in a climbing gym?

Look up and stay clear of the padded safety mats directly underneath someone who is currently on the wall.

Is there a weight limit for rock climbing?

No, gyms accommodate a wide range of body types, though individuals carrying more weight will simply need to focus extra attention on efficient footwork to manage fatigue.

Is there an age limit for rock climbing?

Climbing is a lifelong sport accessible to toddlers as young as 4 years old up to active seniors well into their 70's and 80's.
