OUR SERVICES

Climbing, Fitness, and Community

At Pump Factory, we offer a variety of services to support your climbing journey. From beginner-friendly routes to advanced training programs, our team is here to help you reach your goals.

Climbing

Explore our diverse climbing routes, designed for all skill levels. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced climber, you'll find challenges that suit your abilities.

Fitness Center

Our fitness center is equipped with state-of-the-art equipment to help you build strength, endurance, and flexibility, complementing your climbing experience.

Yoga Classes

Join our yoga classes to improve your balance, flexibility, and mental focus. Our experienced instructors guide you through sessions that enhance your overall well-being.

Personal Training

Work one-on-one with a coach to sharpen your technique, plan your training, and reach specific climbing or fitness goals faster.

Kids Climbing Program

A fun, safe introduction to bouldering for young climbers, building confidence, coordination, and a love for the sport.

Route Setting Workshops

Learn how routes are designed and built, straight from our setters, and get hands-on practice reading and creating problems.

Perfect for Beginners

First Time Pass

Starting your climbing journey? Our First Time Pass is designed specifically for new climbers who want to experience climbing at Pump Factory without commitment. It's the perfect introduction to our gym, our community, and the sport of bouldering.

Benefits Include:

  • ✓ Get two weeks of unlimited climbing and access to all classes and gear upon your first visit.
  • ✓ Safety orientation and proper climbing techniques
  • ✓ Comfortable introduction to our gym environment
  • ✓ Affordable price point for first-time climbers
  • ✓ No equipment rental fees included
  • ✓ Discount voucher for your next visit

Ready to start climbing? Book your First Time Pass today and discover why Pump Factory is the best place to begin your climbing adventure!
